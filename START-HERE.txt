PhoneController: use your phone as a game controller or mouse for your Windows PC
==================================================================================

INSTALL
1. Double-click Install.exe. It installs everything and starts the app.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
     (The app isn't commercially code-signed.)
   - Say Yes when Windows asks for permission. This lets phones connect and installs
     the controller driver.
2. Next time, use the PhoneController shortcut on your Desktop.

CONNECT YOUR PHONE (no internet or router needed)
On the laptop, Wi-Fi must be switched on, but it doesn't need to be connected to anything.
In aeroplane mode, click "Turn on Wi-Fi" in the PhoneController window.
1. In the PhoneController window, scan the "Step 1" code with the phone camera and tap Join.
   If the phone warns "no internet", choose to stay connected.
   If you have mobile data on and the next step won't load, turn mobile data off.
2. Scan the "Step 2" code, or type the address shown under it in the phone's browser.

USB CABLE INSTEAD
1. Plug the phone in. On the phone turn on USB tethering:
   Settings > Connections > Mobile Hotspot and Tethering > USB tethering
2. Click "USB cable" in the PhoneController window and scan the code.

USE THE PHONE AS A TRACKPAD
1. Turn on "Phone can be the mouse" in the PhoneController window.
2. On the phone open Mouse and use it as a trackpad:
   One finger: move.  Tap: click.  Double-tap and keep your finger down: drag.
   Two fingers: tap to right-click, drag to scroll.
   Three fingers: tap to show open windows, swipe up to show the desktop,
   swipe left or right to change desktops.
   Change the speed under the Mouse tab on the phone's home screen.

CLOSING IT
Close the PhoneController window. Phones disconnect, the hotspot goes away and nothing
keeps running in the background.

REMOVE
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > PhoneController > Uninstall.
